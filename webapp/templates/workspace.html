Exercise guide

{% if not is_facilitator %}
Your objective

Investigate, prioritise and document the activity presented during the five-hour exercise. Work as a team and produce an evidence-based incident report; the application does not score your answers.

Working agreement

  • New incidents arrive during the exercise without an advance schedule. Set priorities and divide work deliberately.
  • Use only the evidence available in TabletopSIEM and FreshService. Do not upload queries, results, screenshots or exercise material to Copilot, ChatGPT or another external AI service.
  • Team Alpha and Team Bravo have separate decisions, notes, bookmarks and exports.
  • Raise genuine application or connectivity problems with the facilitator. Technical failures are not exercise injects unless the facilitator says otherwise.

Incident workflow

1 · NewUntriaged and unassigned.
2 · ActiveAcknowledge it, assign ownership and investigate.
3 · ClosedChoose a classification and record evidence-based closure notes.

An incident must move through Active before it can be closed. Closure notes should explain what happened, the evidence considered, and any action or recommendation.

Closure classifications

True positive — Suspicious activityEvidence supports genuine malicious or unauthorised activity.
Benign positive — Suspicious but expectedThe rule worked correctly, but the observed activity was legitimate or approved.
False positive — Incorrect alert logicValid source data was interpreted incorrectly by the detection logic.
False positive — Inaccurate dataIncorrect or misleading source data caused the alert.
UndeterminedAvailable evidence is insufficient for a defensible conclusion.

Facilitator objective

Operate the timed simulation, preserve independent team decisions and capture observations without coaching participants toward the narrative.

Before admitting participants

  1. Run System readiness and resolve every failed check.
  2. Confirm the clean-state backup is current and Kusto reports all expected tables and historical batches.
  3. Open Team Alpha and Team Bravo in separate browser profiles and confirm their access codes, incident history, Logs, Analytics and FreshService views.
  4. Keep the event-day runbook and recovery instructions available outside the application.

Exercise controls

StartStarts the server-owned five-hour clock and timed ingestion. Start is available once per clean run.
Pause / ResumeUse for genuine operational interruptions. Elapsed exercise time is preserved.
End & lockFreezes both teams' official submissions and prevents further decisions, notes, bookmarks or evidence exports. Debrief queries remain available.
UnlockReopens both submissions in a paused state if the facilitator ended the exercise accidentally.
ResetClears all day-of team decisions, notes, bookmarks, observations and activity. Use only after exporting or confirming recovery.

Monitoring and marking

  • The facilitator view shows each team’s status, ownership, classification and closure notes independently.
  • Add private observations for positive practice, coaching points, decisions and technical issues. Participants cannot see them.
  • The activity audit records queries, ticket access, bookmarks, journal activity and accountable evidence exports.
  • Use Export grading pack at the end to download both team reports, facilitator observations, incident decisions and raw exercise data.
  • No correctness or score is displayed to participants during the exercise.

Operational guardrails

  • Do not identify red herrings, confirm hypotheses or disclose future alert timing.
  • For a genuine fault, pause only when participant fairness is affected and record a Technical note.
  • Do not reset a live exercise as a troubleshooting step. Follow the recovery runbook and retain the state database first.
  • At exercise end, capture final observations, choose End & lock, export the grading pack and verify the automatic state backup.
